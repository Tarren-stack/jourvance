# Klaviyo platform beyond the email editor and flows (researched 2026-09-24)

Scope is shipped product surface a Shopify email team would compare against, excluding the drag-and-drop editor and flow-canvas mechanics except where a feature is a distinct product (back in stock, price drop, sunset, A/B inside flows). Labels: **core** = documented in help without an add-on gate; **add-on** = Marketing Analytics (MA) and/or Advanced Klaviyo Data Platform (Advanced KDP / AKDP); **beta / preview** = Klaviyo’s own wording. Article “Updated” dates are the help-center timestamps in search results, not proof the feature launched that day. Secondary blogs are labeled as such and are weaker than help.klaviyo.com.

## Campaigns: A/B, Smart Send Time, include/exclude, resend, throttling, UTM, follow-up, omnichannel

### Takeaway
Campaigns are a core multi-channel send product (email, SMS, push, plus RCS and WhatsApp in newer docs) with subject/content/send-time A/B, list-and-segment include/exclude, UTM, local-time sends, user-controlled gradual send, and an email-only one-click follow-up to non-openers. Smart Send Time is a separate core experiment (not a per-profile STO toggle) that needs at least 12,000 recipients and cannot be combined with A/B. True send throttling in the help docs is a plan-limit failure mode, not a reputation warmup dial.

### Cited Findings
- **Core — campaign A/B (email).** Variations can test subject lines, content, or send times; only one variable should change. More than two variations are possible via Clone; Klaviyo recommends two. Send-time tests keep content and subject identical. Subject-line variations can be generated with AI. Winning ideas called out include preview text, from name, and emoji in the subject. — [How to A/B test an email campaign](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005228148) (updated 2025-08-04)
- **Core — campaign A/B (SMS).** Compare one aspect at a time (SMS vs MMS, emoji, length, language, CTA, image, or send time). Send-time tests require a location/country segment and identical content. Winner metric is click rate or placed order rate; after the test window Klaviyo can send the overall winner or a **personalized** variation predicted from historical engagement, customer lifetime value, and location. Links should use the Klaviyo link shortener; MMS/GIF guidance is under 600 KB. Recipient local timezone is a schedule option. — [How to A/B test SMS campaigns](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406796460443) (updated 2026-09-09)
- **Core — Smart Send Time (Klaviyo’s STO).** Three phases per test: Exploratory (at least 12,000 recipients, Smart Send Time > Exploratory, spread so the model sees a 24-hour window), Focused (best exploratory hour plus two hours before and after), Optimal (each recipient’s local time). Accounts under 12,000 recipients are not eligible. Exploratory-send counts scale down as list size grows (12k–17,999 needs 4–5; over 72,000 needs 1). Reporting shows phase, last send, and open rate per hour. The model waits until after the conversion window before an open-then-order affects the ideal time. A/B tests cannot be combined with Smart Send Time. Intended for wide, non-time-sensitive mail (example: a weekly newsletter). Help also suggests turning Smart Sending off for the exploratory send so the same people can be measured. — [Understanding Smart Send Time](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360029794371) (updated 2026-03-25); [How to perform and analyze Smart Send Time tests](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049073691) (updated 2025-07-06); [Understanding Smart Send Time results](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041411132) (updated 2025-07-06)
- **Core — audience include / exclude.** Send to one or more lists or segments, maximum **15 groups** per campaign; profiles in more than one included group are deduped. Optional **Don’t send to** excludes any number of lists or segments (unengaged, already-notified VIPs, overlapping audiences). Estimated recipient count is shown in the wizard. To reach more than 15 groups, clone the campaign. — [How to exclude lists or segments from a campaign send](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360007016571) (updated 2025-08-04); [How to send a campaign to multiple lists or segments](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005227808)
- **Core — resend / follow-up to non-openers (email only).** On the Review step, **Add a follow-up email** (new or unsent campaigns only). Automatic follow-up is email-only: SMS opens are not detectable and resending SMS is not recommended. Automatic follow-up is **not** available on A/B campaigns. Manual path: segment Received Email ≥1 for that campaign name AND Opened Email = 0, clone the campaign onto that segment, turn off Skip recently emailed profiles if the resend is inside the Smart Sending window, and if scheduled choose **Determine recipients at send time**. — [How to include a follow-up email to resend a campaign to non-openers](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005257928) (updated 2025-08-05)
- **Core — schedule strategies, including gradual send (the user-facing pace control).** Send now, or schedule with Fixed send time (all channels; off-peak minutes such as 3:04 rather than 3:00 are called out as faster), **Gradual send** (batches every minute or every hour, 1%–50% of recipients per batch; 1% per minute ≈ 100 minutes; used to smooth site traffic or pace for deliverability; **cannot** combine with A/B), or Smart Send Time. Recipient local timezone appears for A/B tests when the split is 50/50: a 3 p.m. local send hits California at 3 p.m. PT and the UK at 3 p.m. local. — [Understanding campaign schedule and send options](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050216012) (updated 2026-08-10)
- **Core but not a feature toggle — email throttling.** Help uses “throttling” for sends that are cut because of plan volume limits or unexpectedly high recipient counts. Skipped people are not auto-retried. Mitigations: flexible sending, auto-upgrade, schedule at least one hour of lead time per million recipients, or split the audience. Recovery is manual: segment people who received the campaign, clone, and send the remainder (upgrade first if still over the limit). — [Understanding email throttling](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/9755086953371) (updated 2025-07-06)
- **Core — Smart Sending (frequency cap, separate from Smart Send Time).** Account-level window. Per campaign, step 1 can skip profiles who recently received an email; SMS can separately skip recent campaign SMS and recent flow SMS; push can skip recent push. Skipped messages are not rescheduled. The timer starts for everyone Klaviyo attempted, including bounces and carrier filters, and does not reset for people who were excluded. A/B tests whose entire pool is skipped by Smart Sending are auto-cancelled. SMS and push skipped by Smart Sending cannot be bulk-resent except manually. — [Understanding Smart Sending](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002779311)
- **Core — UTM.** Settings > Other > UTM tracking. Toggles per parameter, separate campaign vs flow values, and **Automatically add UTM parameters to links**. A single campaign or flow message can opt out. As of **2025-06-02**, default `utm_medium` changed from `campaign` / `flow` to **Message type** (email, SMS, WhatsApp, push). Global edits rewrite messages that still use global settings. — [Understanding UTM tracking in Klaviyo](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005247808) (updated 2026-01-13)
- **Core campaign type — omnichannel.** A campaign can target several channels. Documented channels for this surface: email, SMS, RCS, WhatsApp, and push. Per-message **audience filters** narrow the parent campaign audience (engagement, channel affinity such as “email first,” AND/OR). Filters are independent per message, so a profile can receive none or more than one message if filters overlap or leave gaps. — [Understanding Audience Filters in Omnichannel Campaigns](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/44273596909595) (updated 2025-12-11); [Improve email campaign performance with Audience Optimization](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/48320692341019) (updated 2026-06-22)
- **Add-on — Audience Optimization on campaigns.** Available only on **Marketing Analytics or Advanced KDP**. Applies to email, SMS, RCS, WhatsApp, push, and omnichannel. At send time a model drops the highest unsubscribe-risk profiles for that campaign only (not a permanent suppression). Omnichannel-level enablement defaults on for every channel in the campaign and can be turned off per channel. — [Audience Optimization](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/48320692341019) (updated 2026-06-22)

### Inferences
- “STO” in other ESPs maps to Smart Send Time, but it is an account-level multi-send experiment with a 12k floor, not a one-click optimal hour on every campaign and not a per-recipient learned time until the Optimal phase.
- The practical pacing control product teams should copy is Gradual send (1–50% per minute or hour). “Throttling” in Klaviyo docs is a billing/volume safety stop.
- Follow-up-to-non-openers is a first-class email campaign setting, with a manual segment+clone path when A/B, Smart Sending, or SMS gets in the way. Open-based follow-up is structurally impossible for SMS in their model.
- Omnichannel here means one campaign object fanning out to email/SMS/RCS/WhatsApp/push with per-message filters, not a single rendered message.

### Gaps
- No help page in this pass confirmed a campaign-level “inbox provider throttle” or automated warmup ramp distinct from Gradual send and plan-limit throttling.
- Personalized variation selection was documented for SMS A/B (updated 2026-09-09). Whether the same personalized winner exists for email A/B was not confirmed.
- The older “choose a channel (email, SMS, or push)” wizard line was not re-fetched against the 2025–2026 omnichannel articles, so the exact UI for starting an omnichannel campaign (one object vs channel picker) is not fully described.
- RCS and WhatsApp campaign-editor limits (length, media, A/B) were not opened.

## Segments and profiles: conditions, RFM, consent, starred, timeline, merge, suppress, DOI, privacy, SMS keywords, quiet hours

### Takeaway
The segment builder is core and deep: up to 100 conditions covering behavior, profile properties, geography, consent (including subscribe method), and predictive scores once an account has enough orders. RFM groups are real profile properties, but the RFM analysis UI sits under Marketing Analytics or Advanced KDP. Consent, double opt-in, global email suppression (including hard bounce and seven soft bounces), sunset-style list cleaning, SMS STOP-class keywords, and quiet hours are core. Starred segments, profile merge, and a GDPR/CCPA self-serve export/delete workflow were not confirmed in help articles opened here.

### Cited Findings
- **Core — condition library.** Up to 100 conditions per segment: actions, profile properties, geography, and predictive analytics. Consent filter pattern: channel consent plus **because person > subscribed**. Subscribe method can be **Klaviyo form**, with optional form ID. A “cannot receive email marketing” OR “can receive because never subscribed” definition is the documented not-opted-in segment; SMS/push equivalent is simply “cannot receive” that channel. — [Segment conditions reference](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005062847) (updated 2026-03-18); [How to create a not-opted-in segment](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360024584671) (updated 2025-07-06)
- **Core data, add-on report — predictive analytics.** Condition group **Predictive analytics about someone** supports CLV and predicted gender (first name + census data → likely male, likely female, or uncertain). The help page says the full option list appears only if at least **500 customers have placed an order** (buyers, not active profiles). If the block is on a profile but blank, that person lacks enough data. CLV itself is further gated by “criteria outlined in this article,” which was not opened. The conditions overview also says predictive analytics can target likelihood to purchase. — [Segment conditions reference](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005062847) (updated 2026-03-18)
- **Add-on UI — RFM.** Scores are 1–3 for recency, frequency, and monetary value. Help’s recency bands: 3 = last purchase within 180 days, 2 = within 365, 1 = over 365. Frequency and monetary are tertiles (frequency examples: 3+ / 2 / 1 purchase). Groups named in the official scoring article: Champions, Loyal, Recent, Needs Attention, At Risk, Inactive. Segments use properties about someone (academy: `$current_month_rfm_group` and `$previous_month_rfm_group`). Report path is **Advanced KDP > Intelligence > Customer insights > RFM analysis** or **Marketing Analytics > Customer insights > RFM analysis**, with Create segment from the card. RFM membership ignores marketing consent; flows still need a not-suppressed filter. Academy (Marketing Analytics course) recommends transition segments such as Became Needs attention and Became At risk. — [How to build a segment using RFM properties](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/18193920339483) (updated 2025-11-17); [RFM scoring and groups](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/17797937793179) (updated 2025-11-17); [Academy: RFM analysis](https://academy.klaviyo.com/en-us/courses/getting-started-with-marketing-analytics/lessons/rfm-analysis)
- **Conflict on RFM packaging.** A May 2026 agency post claims the one-click RFM dashboard is Advanced-only and that any plan can hand-build RFM-style segments. Klaviyo help lists both Marketing Analytics and Advanced KDP as paths to the RFM screen. Prefer the help article. Group names in that agency post (Potential Loyalists, Cannot Lose Them, Lost) do **not** match the help-center group list above. — [Purposeful Profits, 2026-05-23](https://purposefulprofits.co/blog/klaviyo-rfm-segmentation-dtc-email-revenue); contradicted on packaging and labels by [RFM segment article](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/18193920339483)
- **Core — custom properties.** Sunset flow uses an Update profile property action (example boolean `Unengaged` = true) and segments on **Properties about someone**. — [How to create a sunset flow](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017518492) (updated 2026-03-10)
- **Core — suppression.** Global email suppression: the profile remains and can still sit on lists, but campaigns skip them. List unsubscribe removes them from that list; flow or segment-campaign unsubscribe global-suppresses. Removing suppressed profiles from a list (**Manage List > Remove suppressed profiles**) does not change SMS consent. Manual suppression writes a *Manually Suppressed from email marketing* event on the profile activity log. Automatic suppressions include a hard bounce. Unsuppress of a list/segment cannot clear deliverability suppressions: hard bounce or **7 consecutive soft bounces** stay suppressed. Bulk tools include **Suppression Recommendations**: created ≥365 days ago, received 5+ emails over ~72 weeks, never opened or clicked, never visited the site or purchased. Profiles with a human click or order in the last ~6 months are excluded from those recommendations. — [Remove suppressed profiles from a list](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005077307) (updated 2025-09-22); [Active profile management](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/24263920096027) (updated 2026-03-29); [Manage email suppressions and delete profiles in bulk](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/24312135764251) (updated 2026-03-10)
- **Core — double opt-in.** List setting, on by default in the form guidance. Confirmed subscribers are the only ones added to the list and eligible for the welcome series. Single opt-in is a per-list Consent checkbox and then applies to email, SMS, or both signups to that list with no confirmation. Unconfirmed double opt-in still creates a profile with form properties but does not add the list. Single opt-in is explicitly not recommended for SMS; some carriers and US/Canada abandoned-cart SMS require double opt-in. — [Understanding the double opt-in process](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005251108); [US SMS compliance](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035055312) (updated 2026-09-09)
- **Core — SMS consent keywords.** Opt-out keywords that strip SMS consent: **STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT**. Consent stays off until they resubscribe. Subscribe keywords (example given: **JOIN**) are separate from compliance keywords (HELP, STOP, START). Other automatic consent removal: number change/reassignment, landline, known litigator (can be turned off, with legal-risk warning), carrier blocks. Opt-out language in the message must include STOP in some jurisdictions. — [When Klaviyo removes SMS consent automatically](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4404710011035) (updated 2025-07-06); [SMS settings](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035285472); [GDPR and SMS in Europe](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/18410569130779) (updated 2025-08-31)
- **Core — quiet hours.** Flow SMS quiet hours default **8 p.m.–11 a.m.** (US/Canada Eastern, UK BST, Australia Eastern) based on phone number (US/Canada area code; state-level US rules can be enabled). Outside US/Canada, country eastern time is used. The 2026-09-09 quiet-hours article says the flow default labeled “Use Profile location” still uses area code. Quiet hours are off by default for messages marked transactional but can be enabled. They do **not** apply to default keyword replies (Yes, Stop, Help); they **do** apply to custom subscribe-keyword messages. First message of an SMS welcome series and Shopify post-purchase flows are called out as exceptions. Campaign SMS is also held: US compliance doc says Klaviyo prevents campaign texts before 11 a.m. and after 8 p.m. Eastern, and the product warns if a campaign would violate quiet hours. Per-message checkbox can disable quiet hours on a flow SMS. — [SMS settings](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035285472); [Text message quiet hours in flows](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408737146651) (updated 2026-09-09); [US SMS compliance](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035055312) (updated 2026-09-09)
- **Core — Shopify events as segment/flow fuel (partial).** Placed Order can be filtered by discount code (static or one specific unique code, not by unique-coupon prefix). Ordered Product, Viewed Product, and Started Checkout are required inputs for price-drop logic. — [Getting started with coupon codes](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005084727) (updated 2026-03-17); [How to create a price drop flow](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4404249033755) (updated 2026-02-12)

### Inferences
- Consent is a first-class segment dimension (“can or cannot receive” plus reason and method), not only a list membership check. That is the right primitive for “email subscribed but not SMS.”
- Suppression is email-global and mostly sticky for bounces; SMS consent is a separate bit with its own keywords. Cleaning one does not clean the other.
- RFM should be treated as an add-on analytics surface that writes properties any campaign can use, not as a base-plan dashboard. Hand-built recency/frequency/spend segments remain possible without the report.
- “Activity log” is confirmed at least for suppression events. A full profile timeline product was not documented in the pages retrieved.

### Gaps
- **Starred segments:** no help hit in this pass.
- **Profile merge:** no help hit.
- **GDPR/CCPA access export and erasure workflow:** bulk delete is documented on the suppressions article, but a privacy-request export/delete UI, CCPA-specific flow, or timeline-to-erasure behavior was not opened. Do not assume a one-click DSAR tool.
- **Full predictive catalog** (expected next order date, churn risk, predicted spend) is implied by “likelihood to purchase” and third-party reviews, but the only properties quoted from the current conditions article are CLV and predicted gender, plus the 500-buyer gate. Churn probability as a named segment field is **not** confirmed here.
- Engagement-condition examples beyond Received/Opened Email (clicks, site visits, Shopify “Added to Cart”) were not enumerated from the conditions article body.
- Whether quiet hours also wrap email or push was not stated; docs reviewed are SMS-specific.

## Signup forms: types, targeting, A/B, SMS+email, coupons, spin-to-win

### Takeaway
Sign-up forms are a core onsite product with popup, flyout, and full-page types confirmed, plus teasers, multi-step success states, and a spin-to-win block. Targeting covers new vs existing profiles, URL, UTM, location, device, cart value/contents, exit, scroll, and delay. Form-level A/B (including offers) and both static and Shopify unique coupons on the success step are core. SMS-only forms cannot use unique coupons. Embed and banner were not confirmed by name.

### Cited Findings
- **Core — form types named in help.** Targeting/styles guidance recommends **popup, flyout, or full page**. A **teaser** can show before the form and after close. Steps include a distinct **Success** step after submit. — [Tiered discounts with sign-up forms](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360034336572); [Targeting and behavior settings](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4413544555547) (updated 2026-03-30); [Coupons on sign-up forms](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/6038674938523) (updated 2026-03-30)
- **Core — targeting and behavior.** Rules can use visitor status, URLs, UTM parameters, location, and cart contents (total value, item count, or specific products). Timing: immediate, time delay, scroll distance, exit. Frequency: show again after N days; hide after submit. Device: desktop, mobile, or both. Sample recipes in the help article: welcome form immediate, both devices, every 90 days, **don’t show to existing Klaviyo profiles**; welcome-with-offer uses a teaser, ~15 seconds and 30% scroll, 90-day redisplay, exclude existing profiles; exit-intent, 30 days after close, hide after submit; SMS form aimed at existing email subscribers, 3 seconds after load. Forms can be restricted to a Klaviyo segment and can exclude people already on a list. — [Targeting and behavior settings](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4413544555547) (updated 2026-03-30); [Tiered discounts](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360034336572)
- **Core — form A/B.** Offer tests: two variations, hidden profile property recording which offer was shown, traffic split and winner selection, **Publish A/B test**. Help also tells readers to A/B test a spin-to-win form against a standard consent form. — [How to A/B test offers using sign-up forms](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058920872) (updated 2025-07-06); [Spin-to-win sign-up form](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/34614633330075) (updated 2025-11-06)
- **Core — email and SMS capture.** Same form stack submits to a list; single vs double opt-in follows that list. There is a documented pattern for an SMS form shown to people who already have email. SMS consent copy and a phone field are part of Customer Hub as well, but that is a different surface. — [Double opt-in](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005251108); [Targeting and behavior settings](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4413544555547); [Customer Hub consent](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/35094079400219) (updated 2026-03-13)
- **Core — coupon on submit.** Drag a Coupon block onto the Success step; static code typed in, or unique coupon. Shopify guidance: unique coupons can be set to apply in the cart at checkout (stated in the forms article AI overview). Success-step copy should change if the list is double opt-in. **SMS-only consent forms: static coupons only.** Dynamic codes are cached so the same browser does not farm multiple unique codes for one coupon. Spin-to-win is an Add-block wheel; each slice’s success step gets its own coupon; outcome can be segmented for follow-up flows. — [Coupons on sign-up forms](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/6038674938523) (updated 2026-03-30); [Spin-to-win](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/34614633330075) (updated 2025-11-06); [Tiered discounts](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360034336572)
- **Core — list growth analytics hook.** Benchmarks include a Sign-up Forms page (see analytics section), so form submit performance is a reported surface, not only an editor. — [Benchmarks](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051110431) (updated 2025-10-20)

### Inferences
- Multi-step is real (content step → success step, and spin-to-win branches into per-prize success steps) even though a form type literally named “multi-step” was not quoted.
- Teaser is a behavior/style attached to a form, not a fifth standalone campaign type.
- Gamification (spin-to-win) is in the product, not a third-party embed, as of the Nov 2025 help article.

### Gaps
- **Embed** and **banner** form types: not named in the articles retrieved. Do not treat them as confirmed.
- Exit-intent is documented as a trigger. Scroll depth and delay are documented. Cart-value targeting is documented at summary level; the exact operators (greater than, currency, item SKU) were not quoted line by line.
- Whether form A/B can test timing, teaser, or design rather than only content/offers was not isolated; the offers article is specifically about two offers plus a hidden property. Spin-to-win copy says “consider A/B testing” against a standard form but does not specify the control UI.
- No plan gate was stated for forms, teasers, or spin-to-win.

## Deliverability: domain, branded links, BIMI, Postmaster, bounces, sunset, list cleaning, inbox tests, Smart Sending

### Takeaway
Core deliverability setup is a branded (dedicated) sending domain with SPF/DKIM and DMARC alignment, optional branded click-tracking domain, and bounce-based auto-suppression. BIMI is documented as a DNS project the customer does outside Klaviyo (Validity’s steps), not an in-app toggle. List cleaning is a sunset flow plus Never Engaged segment plus bulk suppress, with a newer recommendation queue. A Deliverability hub under Analytics covers email and SMS health. Google Postmaster and inbox placement tests were not confirmed. Smart Sending is the account-level frequency cap (detailed under campaigns).

### Cited Findings
- **Core — branded / dedicated sending domain.** Klaviyo’s term in current help is **branded sending domain**, also titled dedicated vs shared. Shared sending shows “via klaviyomail.com”; branded sending removes that, isolates reputation, and is described as part of bulk-sender requirements. It is what makes DKIM sign as the brand and what allows DMARC alignment between From address (`sales@example.com`) and sending host (`send.example.com`). Setup is 3 CNAME **or** NS records plus one TXT verification record. Dynamic routing uses NS (`ns1–ns4.klaviyo.com`); static uses CNAMEs. Apply domain, then up to 48 hours for DNS. Existing senders do not have to re-warm if the domain is ≥30 days old and has already been used to send (prior ESP or Klaviyo From address). Return-path is Klaviyo-managed so SPF works by default. — [Branded vs shared sending domains](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/7674941873947); [Domains that affect deliverability](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/15268721014811) (updated 2025-07-06); [Troubleshooting branded sending domain](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4417768780827); [Academy: authenticate with a branded sending domain](https://academy.klaviyo.com/en-us/quick-guides/authenticate-your-emails-with-a-branded-sending-domain)
- **Core — branded links (click-tracking domain).** Help lists a **Click Tracking Domain**: a dedicated domain so tracking links show the brand instead of a Klaviyo host, aimed at trust and click rate. This is distinct from the sending domain. Exact DNS steps were not opened. — [Domains that affect deliverability](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/15268721014811) (updated 2025-07-06)
- **Core guidance, not an in-product switch — BIMI.** Requires SPF, DKIM, and DMARC at `p=quarantine` or `p=reject`. Logo must be square SVG, centered, no extra text, HTTPS, ≤32 KB. Gmail blue check needs a Verified Mark Certificate (trademark). Klaviyo does not host the BIMI record workflow; after prerequisites, follow **Validity’s** implementation steps. Academy calls BIMI optional and says it cannot be turned on while DMARC `p=none`. — [About BIMI](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406592127515) (updated 2025-09-30); [Academy branded domain guide](https://academy.klaviyo.com/en-us/quick-guides/authenticate-your-emails-with-a-branded-sending-domain)
- **Core — bounces and complaints (partial).** Hard bounce auto-suppresses. Seven consecutive soft bounces also suppress, and those suppressions cannot be lifted in the bulk unsuppress tool. Complaint handling as a distinct dashboard or auto-suppress rule was **not** in the articles retrieved. — [Active profile management](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/24263920096027) (updated 2026-03-29); [Bulk suppressions](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/24312135764251) (updated 2026-03-10)

## Analytics and attribution: revenue, opens, MPP, multi-touch, benchmarks, holdout, custom reports, cohorts — base vs Marketing Analytics

### Takeaway
Base analytics include campaign and flow performance, last-touch attribution with editable windows, a model-comparison preview, custom reports, Apple MPP open splits, bot-click reporting, benchmarks (after a send-volume minimum), and the deliverability hub. Marketing Analytics and/or Advanced KDP add linear multi-touch, Active on Site and non-Klaviyo touchpoints (omnichannel attribution), funnel and cohort reports, RFM and CLV dashboards, catalog insights, Audience Optimization, and a beta Report builder. A native incrementality/holdout product was not found. Custom metrics exist on all plans but jump from 1 to 50 only on Advanced KDP. MA and Advanced KDP are mutually exclusive subscriptions.

### Cited Findings
- **Core — default attribution.** New accounts use **last touch**. Default windows: email clicks 5 days, email opens 5 days, SMS clicks 5 days, SMS opens 1 day, SMS deliveries 12 hours, push opens 24 hours, WhatsApp clicks 5 days, WhatsApp opens 12 hours. **Active on Site, 1 day, is listed only for Advanced KDP and Marketing Analytics customers.** Changing settings rewrites attribution historically. UI: **Advanced > Marketing analytics > Attribution**. Editing the price rule **in Shopify** can change already-sent codes even if Klaviyo does not show the edit. Email can include more than one coupon; SMS/MMS only one, via `{% coupon_code 'CouponName' %}`. Trigger metric is **Subscribed to Back in Stock**, not a generic custom metric. Waiting profiles are notified when inventory returns; the flow has a back-in-stock delay component that other metric flows do not. Past-profile backfill generally does not add new waiters. SMS back-in-stock requires SMS to be on, an SMS subscriber, and an inventory-aware catalog; Shopify is in that SMS list. Help also mentions back-in-stock reports, scheduled email summaries, and CSV export of who is queued. ## Plan gates that actually fence a feature

### Takeaway
Do not treat branded sending, forms, segments, campaigns, A/B, Smart Send Time, UTM, back-in-stock, price-drop, or Shopify unique coupons as paid add-ons; help does not put a package name on them. The fences that are explicit: Marketing Analytics (profile add-on, example price $100/month up to 13,500 profiles, requires email and profiles plans), Advanced KDP (total-profile add-on, mutually exclusive with MA), Audience Optimization and several analytics modules on one or both of those, custom metrics capped at 1 unless Advanced KDP (50), Smart Send Time eligibility at 12,000 recipients, email throttling when plan send volume is exceeded, and Customer Hub SMS consent only with an SMS plan.

### Cited Findings
- **Marketing Analytics add-on.** Not in base email + profiles. Needs both an email plan and a profiles plan. Example price in help: from $100/month up to 13,500 profiles. Must cancel Advanced KDP before adding MA. — [Marketing Analytics getting started](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/33789259613595); [Billing](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037347851)
- **Advanced KDP add-on.** Not in base. Billed on total profiles, not active profiles. Mutually exclusive with MA. Raises custom metrics from 1 to 50. — [Billing](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037347851); [Marketing Analytics getting started](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/33789259613595)
- **Features named as MA and/or Advanced KDP.** Funnel analysis, conversion overview, RFM analysis UI, cohort analysis, customizable CLV, customer insights, catalog insights, predictive models (MA article). Linear multi-touch and Active on Site / omnichannel attribution (academy + attribution help: both packages). Audience Optimization (both). Report builder beta (both, including trials). Cohort report path lists both nav labels. — [MA getting started](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/33789259613595); [Cohorts](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/33789127136027); [Advanced attribution academy](https://academy.klaviyo.com/en-us/courses/getting-started-with-marketing-analytics/lessons/advanced-attribution); [Omnichannel attribution](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/41008338238875); [Audience Optimization](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/48320692341019); [Report builder](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/42789551068699)
- **Volume gates that are not packages.** Smart Send Time: ineligible under 12,000 recipients. Benchmarks: at least 25 emails or SMS in six months. Predictive segment options: at least 500 purchasers for the full list. Email throttling: plan send limits, with flexible sending or auto-upgrade as the escape. Campaign include cap: 15 lists/segments. Segment definition cap: 100 conditions. — [Smart Send Time](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360029794371); [Benchmarks](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051110431); [Segment conditions](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005062847); [Email throttling](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/9755086953371); [Multiple lists](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005227808)

- **Not a Klaviyo SKU.** BIMI needs an external VMC for Gmail. Branded sending domain is a DNS setup, not described as an add-on. — [BIMI](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406592127515); [Branded sending domain](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/7674941873947)

### Inferences
- The packaging story for a competitor matrix is: base Klaviyo already includes the send, segment, form, Shopify merchandising automations, and last-touch reporting. The paid analytics/CDP tier is where Klaviyo puts multi-touch, onsite attribution, RFM/CLV/funnel/cohort, catalog insights, send-time audience risk filtering, and the new report builder.
- Because MA and Advanced KDP cannot coexist, “has RFM” does not tell you which SKU. Feature lists that say “Advanced KDP only” for RFM conflict with current help.

### Gaps
- Full price card (email tier names, SMS prices, reviews price, Customer Hub price, dedicated IP) was intentionally not researched beyond gates above. The $100 MA example is a single help-center figure, not a full rate card, and may not match every profile band.
- Whether Composer, Customer Agent, Customer Hub (aside from SMS), or Klaviyo Reviews require MA, a Shopify app charge, or a higher email tier: not stated in sources opened.
- Flexible sending / auto-upgrade are named as throttling remedies but their plan requirements were not opened.
