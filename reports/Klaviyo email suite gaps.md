# Give imported Klaviyo flows a landing

Klaviyo is a full send-and-automate product; Jourvance already has a smaller one, and the difference is a concrete list of blocks, triggers, and actions rather than a missing vendor. Jourvance can stack five real blocks, fill seven order tokens, and run a flow made of a wait, an email, a text, and a single “ordered since they joined” check. A Klaviyo import keeps waits, emails, and texts, maps one exact placed-order split onto that check, and throws away every other branch. The features a rebuilt flow has nowhere to land are A/B tests, non-order splits, profile-property splits, update-profile, webhooks, clock-time and weekday delays, date and price-drop and back-in-stock triggers, show/hide, Django tags, and product feeds. Those are ordinary React, Express, hub-send, and Shopify Admin work, and they should be saved disabled the same way new flows and letters already are. Predicted CLV, cross-store benchmarks, inbox-placement networks, and Klaviyo’s collaborative “may also like” model do not travel with an export; an honest local stand-in is this store’s own orders, a merchant-chosen hour, and a test sent only to addresses the merchant types.

Three buckets apply to every row below. **Build ourselves** means the current stack can do it: the React editors, `server.mjs` flow compiler and `blocksToHtml`, `hub.email.send`, and the Shopify Admin calls already used for products and checkout links. **Hub wiring** means a `proxyHub` or `hub.email.*` route already exists and the gap is the Jourvance UI or the send/analytics payload, not a new provider. **Do not fake** means the feature is a Klaviyo model or a network this account does not have; the honest local version is named and is not a cloned score. “Confirmed” means a Klaviyo help or developer page is cited inside the note named on that row. “Unconfirmed” means that note’s own gaps section, and those rows are not a spec.

## Jourvance already sends a short suite, and most of it stays off

The owned mail path is real. `cleanBlocks` accepts exactly six kinds, and the builder offers five of them: **heading, text, button, divider, and image**. `html` is stored for a pasted Klaviyo template (cap 60,000 characters) and is not an Add button. Anything else is saved as text. A send can hold 24 blocks. Heading renders as `h1`, text as a paragraph with newlines turned into breaks, divider as `hr`, button as a black link only when the URL is `http` or `https`, and image as `img` for an `http(s)` URL. The footer line is the fixed sentence “Sent by Jourvance for this store.” Tokens are filled only in text, the button label, and the button URL. Image `src`, image `alt`, and raw `html` are not filled, and `html` is sanitized by stripping script tags, `on*` attributes, and `javascript:` ([jourvance-suite.md](file:///Users/tarrenmunoz/antigravity/Local-AI-App-Builder/generated-projects/jourvance/research_notes/Klaviyo%20email%20suite%20gaps/jourvance-suite.md)).

`fillMailTokens` replaces `{{key}}` only for keys the send actually sets: **`first_name`, `order_number`, `order_total`, `currency`, `line_items`, `tracking_line`, and `refund_amount`**. Unknown tokens are deleted on program and flow sends and left in place when the builder previews with `merge: 'keep'`. Drip ticks and campaign sends do not call `fillMailTokens`. The default drip body contains `{{abandoned_checkout_url}}`, and that token is never filled. `{{ unsubscribe_link }}` exists only in clipboard export text. Preview text is stored on campaigns, drips, and canvas steps, and is not passed to `hub.email.send`. Campaign sends also skip token fill, so a broadcast is one body to many addresses ([jourvance-suite.md](file:///Users/tarrenmunoz/antigravity/Local-AI-App-Builder/generated-projects/jourvance/research_notes/Klaviyo%20email%20suite%20gaps/jourvance-suite.md)).

Custom flows have six triggers — `lead_capture`, `exit_intent`, `checkout_abandonment`, `order_paid`, `quiet_buyer`, `manual` — and five node types — `trigger`, `delay`, `email`, `condition`, `sms`. The only condition is `ordered_since_enroll`. Delays are whole hours from 0 to 90 days. There is no A/B node, no re-entry field, and no second incoming path: `compileFlow` throws if a step is reached twice. New flows are created with `enabled: false`. The same off-until-turned-on rule covers four transactional letters (`order_confirmation`, `shipping_confirmation`, `order_cancelled`, `refund`) and two automations (`post_purchase` at 24h and 72h, `winback` after 45 quiet days). `shopifyStillSends` is true, so enabling a letter does not stop Shopify’s own notification. The exception is the drip queue: Welcome (`lead_capture`, 0/24/48h) and cart recovery (`checkout_abandonment`) are inserted if missing, the flow map marks sequences enabled, and leads and checkouts enroll them unless Klaviyo is the sender. That auto-enroll path is existing behavior, not a pattern to copy. Nothing in this report should turn a flow, a letter, or a subscription on ([jourvance-suite.md](file:///Users/tarrenmunoz/antigravity/Local-AI-App-Builder/generated-projects/jourvance/research_notes/Klaviyo%20email%20suite%20gaps/jourvance-suite.md)).

Around that core, Jourvance already proxies the hub for delivery, lint, sender and domain setup, the events webhook, analytics, live image blocks, inbox and inbound parse, SMS status/audience/history/consent/send, subscriber add, and a read-only form list. It does not inject an open pixel, does not append the physical address collected in Sending setup, and does not apply `tagOutboundLinks` (UTM plus cart attributes) to block HTML. Audience segments are six fixed predicates. Signup forms are `popup`, `bar`, or `embed`, created off, on published landing pages, with a popup delay of 0–120 seconds and no other targeting. Catalog data, discount codes, and a countdown exist for landing pages and checkout URLs, not as email blocks ([jourvance-suite.md](file:///Users/tarrenmunoz/antigravity/Local-AI-App-Builder/generated-projects/jourvance/research_notes/Klaviyo%20email%20suite%20gaps/jourvance-suite.md)).

## Import drops every Klaviyo step the canvas cannot store

`flowFromKlaviyo` is the rebuild blocker list. It copies a time delay into `delayHours` (weeks, days, and hours kept; minutes rounded to hours; cap 90 days) and writes that `delay_until_weekdays` was **copied as a plain wait**. It copies an email’s subject and, until a template fetch returns HTML, uses preview text as the body or a sentence that the template was not copied. HTML comes back as one `kind: 'html'` block. SMS body is copied up to 480 characters, and an empty SMS is skipped. A conditional or trigger split becomes the order check only when `splitOrderPolarity` matches exactly one `profile-metric` on Placed Order or Ordered Product, timeframe `flow-start` or empty, and a measurement that means ordered versus not. Every other split keeps one path: the Yes side if both sides send, otherwise whichever side contains a letter or a text. An `ab-test` is one path, with the note “An A/B test was rebuilt as one path. Jourvance does not pick a winner.” Any other action type is noted as left out and the walker continues. A join keeps the shared steps on the first path only. Triggers map added-to-list to `lead_capture`, a metric whose name matches started checkout to `checkout_abandonment`, and placed order or ordered product to `order_paid`. Everything else, including a date, a price drop, low inventory, and back in stock, becomes `manual`. Copies are saved `enabled: false`. Flows already enabled are left unchanged. Archived flows are skipped. The catalog stops at 40. The graph stops at 24 nodes. Notes stop at 16 ([jourvance-suite.md](file:///Users/tarrenmunoz/antigravity/Local-AI-App-Builder/generated-projects/jourvance/research_notes/Klaviyo%20email%20suite%20gaps/jourvance-suite.md)).

That walker cannot keep a feature the compiler will not store. `cleanFlow` forces the condition field to `ordered_since_enroll`, allows yes/no only on conditions, and rejects a second incoming edge, so a rejoin cannot land even if the importer emitted one. Profile filters other than that one order polarity are not copied. Per-message from-address, reply-to, cc, bcc, Smart Sending, transactional, extra filters, and UTM are not copied. Campaigns, segments, forms, and universal content are outside `flowFromKlaviyo` and `syncKlaviyo`. `metricHandoffAllowed` refuses Added to Cart and Viewed Product handoffs into Klaviyo, which is a separate limit from the copy itself ([jourvance-suite.md](file:///Users/tarrenmunoz/antigravity/Local-AI-App-Builder/generated-projects/jourvance/research_notes/Klaviyo%20email%20suite%20gaps/jourvance-suite.md)).

The Klaviyo-side names for those drops are confirmed, not guessed. Message A/B varies subject, preview text, sender name, sender email, and body, with weights, optional shifting distribution, and an automatic winner on click rate (default) or open rate only. Branch A/B is a random-sample split with a human picking the winner. Profile-property and event splits are the June 2026 multi-branch conditions. Update-profile creates, updates, or deletes properties. Webhooks are one POST with a JSON body. Time delays take minutes, hours, or days, plus a clock time, weekdays, and a profile timezone. Date, price drop, and low inventory are trigger types; back in stock is the metric Subscribed to Back in Stock plus a back-in-stock delay. Show/hide is per block or section. Django tags and product feeds are how the dropped creative still refers to people and products ([klaviyo-flows.md](file:///Users/tarrenmunoz/antigravity/Local-AI-App-Builder/generated-projects/jourvance/research_notes/Klaviyo%20email%20suite%20gaps/klaviyo-flows.md), [klaviyo-editor.md](file:///Users/tarrenmunoz/antigravity/Local-AI-App-Builder/generated-projects/jourvance/research_notes/Klaviyo%20email%20suite%20gaps/klaviyo-editor.md)). Until those node types exist and the importer writes them, “rebuild every flow” stays false no matter how complete campaigns or forms become. Rebuilt graphs stay off. The importer must not enroll past profiles and must not invent opens, clicks, or revenue so a winner can be chosen.

## Conclusion

The next import keeps more of the original flow only if the canvas and `flowFromKlaviyo` change together, in this order: minute delays with a clock time, weekdays, and a timezone; a condition node that can hold a profile property, a trigger field, consent, list membership, and a random sample, with up to 20 paths and a legal rejoin; a message A/B that stores every variation and does not crown a winner without a real open or click; update-profile and a JSON POST webhook; show/hide rules and a small Django subset (`if`, `for`, `default`, `lookup`, catalog id, coupon tag) that actually run inside the imported HTML; a product block and a line-item table; and triggers for a merchant-set date, a checkout price drop, and a back-in-stock subscription. Each of those lands on a flow that is still saved disabled. Caps at 40 flows and 24 nodes will keep “every flow” false until they are raised as well.

What makes the suite feel complete for a Shopify merchant, after those landing spots exist, is the mail around the graph: styled blocks (section, columns, split, table, spacer, social, header bar, button and divider styles), preview text that is actually sent, an unsubscribe link and the physical address, Smart Sending and SMS quiet hours in front of the hub, unique Shopify codes minted at send, best-seller and co-purchase feeds from this store’s orders, form targeting and double opt-in, a segment builder, and a campaign that can wait until a clock time or drip out in batches. Leave the network on the other side of the glass. Predicted CLV, churn, gender, expected next order, cross-store benchmarks, inbox seed tests, Audience Optimization, Smart Send Time’s optimal phase, collaborative “may also like,” RCS, WhatsApp, push, AMP, Customer Hub, and Reviews are not gaps to imitate. The honest substitutes are historic spend, days since the last order, a hour the merchant picks, co-purchase on this catalog, lint plus a test send, and a segment the merchant excludes, and none of those substitutes should be written back onto a profile as if Klaviyo had scored it.
