# Jourvance About page story

## Brief and authorization

The owner asked to expand and add his story to the existing About page: Tarren Munoz,
eight years in marketing, experience with many software products, builders, and tools,
and Jourvance bringing together what he learned and needed. This request authorizes
the copy edit; no additional design direction is needed.

Serve business owners and marketers evaluating Jourvance, including people scanning
on a phone, reading in a second language, or navigating by headings. Use the existing
About page's dark surfaces, typography, cards, and Canvas Studio action.

## Direction

Lead with "Built by a marketer. Made for your business." Tell the origin in the
founder's first person, followed by his name and role. Keep growth aspirational,
without invented results, clients, credentials, or guarantees. Use plain language,
short paragraphs, descriptive headings, and no em dash. Adapt the story card's padding
to leave room for the longer text on phones.

Voice: personal, practical, direct. Prefer "tools", "business", "customer journey",
and "follow-up" over technical implementation language. Reading-level target is plain
general-audience copy; no measured reading score is claimed.

## Plan and verification

1. Update the headline, founder story, principles, and supporting call to action in
   `src/components/public/AboutPage.tsx`.
2. Check the rendered story, heading order, responsive fit, and Canvas Studio action
   in Chrome using a credential-free temporary copy of the app.
3. Run `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npm test`, and a Vite build; record actual results and
   omissions in `JOURVANCE_RUNNING_AUDIT.md`.

No new dependency, publication, or change to account data is part of this task.
